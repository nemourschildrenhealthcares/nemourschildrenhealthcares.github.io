Our Vision: Well Beyond Medicine

World-class care is only part of the solution. Children need so much more.

Going Well Beyond Medicine

About 80% of what affects a child’s health takes place in their home, school and community. Nemours Children’s is making a difference with programs that address nonmedical challenges — to create a healthier future, for all kids.

Reimagining Children’s Health

Collaboration Is Key

Pediatric Research

At Nemours Children’s, research goes beyond discovery to drive impact. We study what makes children sick, what helps them heal, and what gives every child the best chance to thrive.

Life-Changing Research Happens Here. We focus on cancer, diabetes, heart disease, mental health, and everyday factors that shape children’s well-being.
